# Independent outside review prompt — WHU PRD/Plan v2

You are the same independent reviewer who reviewed the frozen WHU PRD/Plan v1 packet. Review this
v2 packet independently. Do not assume that a change is adequate merely because the adjudication
says a finding was accepted.

## Question

Does v2 now provide a credible, falsifiable, and economically bounded product-validation plan that
adequately resolves the four v1 blockers and the material sequencing/viability concerns?

In particular, determine whether:

1. the sophisticated non-expert reader is now a real measured outcome, with a mandatory path from
   instrument pilot to frozen confirmatory criteria and a not-ship trigger;
2. the statistical design no longer assumes unsupported independence, and inability to obtain
   enough independent information can itself end the program;
3. the comparator is commercially meaningful, fully specifiable, fairly resourced, independently
   approved, and frozen before use;
4. `severe`, `consequential`, `catastrophic subgroup`, `useful coverage`, `material change`, `false
   assurance`, and `load-bearing` must be operationally defined and independently approved before
   they govern results;
5. the new early Epistemic Feasibility Probe is genuinely a cheap kill test rather than a demo or a
   route to automatic remediation;
6. qualified-reference/evaluator independence is honest about the one-internal-human constraint,
   and reference unavailability or unaffordability produces a real negative/indeterminate result;
7. reader testing and qualified substantive reference testing remain distinct;
8. budget, calendar, retry, full-TCO, reference-maintenance, and invalidation rules can terminate
   the program rather than merely generate more work;
9. naturalistic/planted defects and easy/hard or consequential strata cannot be pooled to hide
   failure;
10. strong v1 features—extraction falsifiability, cross-domain falsifiability, operational/economic
    negative outcomes, SHADOW boundaries, adverse-evidence publication, and the separation of
    process completion from warrant—were preserved; and
11. no production/release machinery or major substrate program is authorized ahead of evidence
    that the epistemic core is promising.

## Review discipline

- Review the primary artifacts, changelog, adjudication, and neutral context in this packet.
- Verify internal consistency and the exact mandatory freeze/failure paths; do not reward the mere
  presence of controls or labels.
- Do not require final numeric empirical thresholds where the design correctly requires a pilot or
  qualified-reference basis, but treat any `TO_BE_FROZEN` item as a defect if it lacks a mandatory
  latest freeze point, independent approval where warranted, or a failure outcome.
- Do not automatically accept the program-envelope options; assess whether the owner-decision and
  exhaustion mechanism is sufficiently bounded and non-gameable.
- Distinguish product-validation defects from ordinary engineering hygiene.
- Source every external factual assertion. Put unsourced concerns in a separate section rather than
  presenting them as findings.
- You may find nothing. Do not manufacture recommendations or comment on style.

## Output

Lead with exactly one disposition:

- `READY_FOR_MERGE_AND_BOUNDED_NEXT_DECISION`
- `READY_WITH_MATERIAL_FIXES`
- `NOT_YET_A_CREDIBLE_PRODUCT_VALIDATION_PLAN`
- `V2_REVEALS_STRUCTURAL_FEASIBILITY_PROBLEM`

Then provide:

1. **Blocker closure:** B-1 through B-4, each `RESOLVED`, `PARTIALLY_RESOLVED`, or `UNRESOLVED`, with
   exact v2 citations.
2. **Material closure:** M-1 through M-7 using the same statuses.
3. **New blocking or material findings:** exact quote/location, severity, why it prevents a
   defensible product verdict, source where external, and the smallest adequate fix.
4. **Gate audit:** whether each Phase A-I has a genuine adverse outcome and whether Phase B can stop
   the program without automatic building.
5. **Reference/reader/comparator/statistical audit:** whether the four instruments are distinct,
   validly sequenced, and realistically staffable under the documented team constraint.
6. **Economics/exhaustion audit:** whether full TCO and budget/calendar/retry limits can actually
   produce an economic or reliability stop.
7. **Remaining owner decisions:** only decisions that cannot responsibly be made in the plan.
8. **Suspicions I could not source**, if any.

Do not contact the project, request internal hostile reviews, or infer facts about the private
implementation beyond what this packet states.
