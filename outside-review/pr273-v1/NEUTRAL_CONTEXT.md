# Neutral Context

WHU is intended to produce and maintain deeply researched assessments for sophisticated nonexperts. Its stated aims include accuracy, fairness, evidentiary breadth, reconstructability, and explicit limits.

Earlier iterative WHU architecture exposed weaknesses in state, provenance, search, semantic integrity, and warrant. The current repository is a professional rebuild intended to make those boundaries explicit and auditable.

The governing methodological boundary is property-specific: bounded independent references can support reliance, but the project has found no general method that establishes correctness when the target property lacks an accessible independent reference. This is a bounded research conclusion, not a claim of impossibility.

WHU does not claim open-world search completeness. Acquisition adequacy is bounded by a declared protocol, scope, routes, evidence classes, stopping rules, missingness analysis, and explicit unresolved states.

Model agreement is not treated as truth or as an independent correctness reference.

Selective human intervention is allowed. Human and expert work must be visible, designed, attributed, and measured rather than treated as a free or hidden input.

The product must be allowed to fail reliability and economic-viability tests. Continued control-building is not itself evidence that the product works.

Aspirin is a development and acceptance case. It is not the universal product schema, and success on aspirin alone cannot establish cross-domain generality.

This context makes no judgment about whether PR #273 satisfies these constraints.
