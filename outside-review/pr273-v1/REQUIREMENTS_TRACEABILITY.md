Status terms:

- **Implemented:** code/schema/tests exist on current main.
- **Staging-evidenced:** a protected staging run/artifact was inspected.
- **Partial:** some objects or tests exist, but end-to-end or operational evidence is missing.
- **Research-only/SHADOW:** cannot confer publication authority.
- **Gap:** no adequate evidence was found; this is not a conclusion that the capability is impossible.

## 4. Current traceability conclusion

The strongest coverage is in mechanical identity, custody primitives, versioning, receipts, dependency invalidation, typed failure states, and authority-boundary design. The weakest coverage is exactly where product viability depends on evidence rather than architecture: open-world acquisition performance, detailed source-grounded extraction, global synthesis warrant, reviewer usability, qualified-reference evaluation, maintenance under change, cross-domain replication, and total operating economics.

No requirement in this matrix should be upgraded from partial to satisfied merely because an adjacent object exists or a workflow completed.
