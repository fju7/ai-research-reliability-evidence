# Canonical Research Constraints

This is a concise navigation aid, not a scorecard. The sources below are canonical artifacts merged into `fju7/what-holds-up` at the PR #273 recorded base, `e56e2d18cddfd2814711f75be36409cbbc4474d1`. The source repository is private; packet recipients without repository access can evaluate the summaries here and request authorized access if source-level inspection is necessary.

## Professional engineering baseline

The [WHU professional reference architecture](https://github.com/fju7/what-holds-up/blob/e56e2d18cddfd2814711f75be36409cbbc4474d1/docs/architecture/whu-professional-reference-architecture.md) defines a small mechanically testable trusted core, replaceable semantic modules, accountable human/institutional authority, and a SHADOW research boundary. Its claim ceiling is auditable, versioned, accountable research-publication infrastructure—not autonomous truth or open-world completeness.

## Epistemic reliance

The [frozen epistemic-reliance state](https://github.com/fju7/what-holds-up/blob/e56e2d18cddfd2814711f75be36409cbbc4474d1/docs/research/epistemic-reliance-state-2026-09.md) concludes that bounded formal, executable, experimental, outcome, and defensible-reference anchors can support reliance for specified properties. It found no generally validated method for justified accuracy claims when no independent human, formal, executable, physical, delayed-outcome, or statistically identifiable reference is accessible. Model agreement, voting, explanation, and self-review are not independent correctness evidence without external calibration.

## Source-acquisition adequacy

The [source-acquisition adequacy synthesis](https://github.com/fju7/what-holds-up/blob/e56e2d18cddfd2814711f75be36409cbbc4474d1/docs/research/source-acquisition-adequacy-final-synthesis.md) concludes that professional methods provide a strong baseline but do not produce universal open-world completeness or evidence-sufficiency certificates. Adequacy must be defended through prospectively declared scope and evidence roles, complementary routes, exact receipts, study/report linkage, bounded recall evidence where possible, independent review, missingness analysis, calibrated conclusions, and updating. Provider count and marginal yield alone are not adequacy evidence.

## Semantic integrity

The [semantic-integrity synthesis](https://github.com/fju7/what-holds-up/blob/e56e2d18cddfd2814711f75be36409cbbc4474d1/docs/research/semantic-integrity-state-of-art-synthesis.md) treats source support and substantive correctness as separate axes. Native-source grounding, exact locators, qualifiers, quantities, modality limits, and explicit unresolved states are needed; locally valid structure or agreement cannot establish substantive correctness.

## Global warrant

The [global-warrant synthesis](https://github.com/fju7/what-holds-up/blob/e56e2d18cddfd2814711f75be36409cbbc4474d1/docs/research/global-warrant-final-synthesis.md) preserves a residual distinction between locally grounded facts and a warranted overall conclusion. Evidence weighting, applicability, bridge assumptions, dependence, alternatives, uncertainty, and descriptive/inferential/normative distinctions require explicit treatment. The document does not claim that the rebuild automatically solves these problems.

## Verification coverage

The [verification-coverage synthesis](https://github.com/fju7/what-holds-up/blob/e56e2d18cddfd2814711f75be36409cbbc4474d1/docs/research/verification-coverage-final-synthesis.md) concludes that a control can correctly establish an encoded property, such as citation support, while leaving a conclusion-dependent property, such as applicability or evidence weighting, unchecked. Structure can become checkbox false assurance; verification accuracy and verification coverage must therefore remain distinct.

## Continuous surveillance

The [continuing-evidence-surveillance ADR](https://github.com/fju7/what-holds-up/blob/e56e2d18cddfd2814711f75be36409cbbc4474d1/docs/architecture/0011-continuing-evidence-surveillance.md) makes maintenance part of the evidence lifecycle. Each cycle must record its coverage, route failures, and newly acquired sources. A no-material-change disposition is bounded to what was detected under the executed protocol through a stated date and cannot imply that no important external evidence exists.

## SHADOW and authority boundaries

The [authority-vocabulary ADR](https://github.com/fju7/what-holds-up/blob/e56e2d18cddfd2814711f75be36409cbbc4474d1/docs/architecture/0012-authority-vocabulary-and-boundary.md) separates mechanical completion, workflow completion, deterministic controls, review, and bounded authorization. SHADOW or invalidated dependencies are ineligible for authorization. `VERIFIED`, `APPROVED`, `SUFFICIENT`, and `AUTHORITATIVE` are not generic production states, and authorization is permission for an exact use—not certification of truth.
