# Project and Method Context

## Chronology

WHU began as a source-backed research workflow for sophisticated nonexperts and generalists. Historical work exposed ordinary engineering weaknesses such as incomplete custody, provenance, versioning, and correction records. Because those weaknesses confound later interpretation, the historical incident corpus was judged inadequate for estimating residual scientific or epistemic failures. Remembered incidents are context only, not data.

A professional rebuild is proceeding separately. PR #30 is a design-only proposal for observation after that rebuild satisfies a signed baseline gate. It is inactive and has no resulting observations.

Merged PR #79 integrated prior WHU research with public work on evidence synthesis, semantic controls, weak-supervisor reliance, and professional assurance. PR #88 then conducted a targeted public-source mapping of current programs and methods, including positive results, negative cases, reference structure, and expert burden.

## PR #88 method

The protocol targeted 10–15 substantively relevant program or method families. It preferred current official sources, primary papers, public-institute or independent-evaluator reports, and disclosed benchmark methods. It required first-party evidence to be distinguishable from independent validation; authors' stated claims to be separated from WHU inferences; strong counterexamples to receive explicit attention; and reference/expert load to be recorded.

The search was targeted and saturation-based, not systematic or exhaustive. Search failure was not treated as proof of novelty. Application claims were classified as direct evidence, analogy, or speculative extension. The source cutoff was 2026-09-16.

## Claim boundaries

- Weak-supervisor and scalable-oversight research is active prior art.
- Strong empirical successes exist in bounded and outcome-gradable settings.
- Many evaluations ultimately rely on hidden answers, executable tests, held-out benchmarks, experiments, bounded corpora, human-calibrated judges, outcomes, or expert-built references.
- Open-domain evidence synthesis is proposed as a reference-poor application region within existing fields, not a separate or novel field.
- The reviewed public corpus does not prove impossibility, exhaustive absence, prevalence, or future failure.
- “Not demonstrated” is limited to the reviewed public evidence and declared intended use.
- Upstream expert investment may remove routine runtime expert review inside a validated envelope.
- WHU has no prospective result. Potential contribution is a measurement proposal, not an established empirical contribution.

## Proposed measurement themes

PR #88 identifies four candidate themes for outside scrutiny: consequential false assurance after user-visible controls pass; recognition of and escalation beyond a validated envelope; expert burden shifted upstream versus retained at runtime; and evidence-selection, applicability, weighting, dependence, and global-warrant failures in a naturalistic workflow.

These are hypotheses about potentially useful measurements. The outside reviewer is asked to determine whether they are coherent, already answered, redundant, unmeasurable, or worth prospective investigation.
