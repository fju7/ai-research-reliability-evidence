# Neutral Summary of the Inactive PR #30 Plan

PR #30 is a preregistration design, not an active study. Its head is `002074d0f2bfce7fb242b8e1b9c3f986462a6a60`. No observation has begun, every activation checkbox is unchecked, and no empirical result exists.

## Proposed gate and sample

Observation could begin only after a separately signed professional-baseline manifest records the repository, schema, deployment, policies, prompts, models, tools, controls, authority boundaries, operational tests, limitations, and independent audit. Material baseline drift would pause accrual and create a new stratum.

The proposed unit is one consecutive eligible research-to-release episode. The initial horizon is 50 eligible episodes or six calendar months, whichever takes longer, with a nine-month administrative maximum. Fewer than 25 eligible episodes by that point would close as cadence-insufficient and support only feasibility and burden reporting.

## Proposed observation and reference

The plan would preserve privacy-governed event records for search, sources, transformations, claims, review, authorization, corrections, versions, burden, and outcomes. Research would not alter ordinary production before its normal terminal state. Discovery channels would include ordinary corrections, user or external challenges, independent post-release review, later source changes, deterministic failures, and a prespecified random audit of apparently clean episodes.

Semantic adjudication would use qualified reviewers, native evidence, independent first passes where feasible, explicit disagreement, and states including unresolved, reference defect, inaccessible, and error. Models could assist organization or triage but would not replace expertise required for the reference.

## Proposed outcomes

The design would descriptively register consequential failures, where they entered, whether visible controls exposed them, discovery channel, correction timing, false-assurance reversals, expert and latency burden, reference uncertainty, and whether simpler professional controls might have sufficed. Selectively discovered cases would not provide prevalence estimates; only the randomly audited population could support a failure proportion, with clustering and uncertainty retained.

## Limitations the reviewer should assess

- one small project's observations cannot establish prevalence across AI systems;
- the plan is observational and cannot estimate control efficacy causally;
- professional rebuild quality, model changes, task mix, and observer effects may confound interpretation;
- sparse consequential failures may yield no stable mechanism;
- expert reference construction may reproduce the very burden under study;
- incomplete or disputed references may leave cases unresolved;
- naturalistic breadth can reduce representativeness, comparability, and statistical power;
- no plan can manufacture an objective oracle for final warrant where reasonable conclusions differ; and
- a simpler expert-assisted workflow may dominate the proposed approach.

The plan explicitly treats low residual failure, ordinary engineering explanations, unstable mechanisms, low yield, excessive burden, unresolved references, or better simpler workflows as valid adverse results. Any later experiment would require a new decision and preregistration.
