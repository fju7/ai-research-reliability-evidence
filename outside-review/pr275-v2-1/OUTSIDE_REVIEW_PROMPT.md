# Independent closure-review prompt — WHU PRD/Plan v2.1

You are the same independent reviewer who reviewed the frozen WHU v1 and v2 packets. This is a
narrow closure review, not another broad architecture review.

## Central question

Have N-1 through N-6 been adequately resolved, and is the validation program now ready for the
owner to perform the costing/necessity analysis and decide whether and when an experimental envelope
should be authorized?

Ask especially:

> Does v2.1 avoid assuming a large external human panel while still refusing to substitute model
> agreement or another invalid anchor where qualified human judgment is genuinely required?

## Closure tests

For each N-1 through N-6 return `RESOLVED`, `PARTIALLY_RESOLVED`, or `UNRESOLVED`, citing exact v2.1
text. Determine whether:

1. every validation function is matched to the strongest defensible anchor/reference class and
   costed low/base/high before envelope selection, without importing your illustrative role-hour,
   rate, headcount, or reader-study estimates as conclusions;
2. a named Phase B reserve freezes before Phase A spending and failure to preserve it stops the
   program;
3. `VALIDATION_NOT_AFFORDABLE_AT_THIS_SCALE` is kept logically separate from
   `PRODUCT_ECONOMICS_IMPLAUSIBLE` throughout outcomes, traceability, and exhaustion;
4. `BOUNDED_SYNTHESIS_CORE_PROMISING` cannot be read as acquisition/evidence-selection adequacy,
   open-world completeness, cross-domain reliability, or production readiness and opens only the
   next minimal phase;
5. only the named independent protocol/statistical approver may accept a role-separation
   equivalence design, the argument freezes before relevant outcomes, and cost, schedule,
   recruitment difficulty/failure, convenience, or model agents cannot establish equivalence;
6. Phase F has real entry criteria, latest freeze points for maintenance and burden rules, pass
   criteria, and stop/fail criteria;
7. system/instrument shared lineage has a material independent challenge path and another model
   family alone is not treated as independence;
8. the human-reference decision point states the exact unvalidated property, exhausted alternative
   anchors, required human role/qualification/independence, source access, disagreement/uncertainty,
   hours/cost/maintenance, decision value, and consequence if the owner declines;
9. qualified humans, if later used, remain bounded fallible references rather than truth oracles;
10. reader and substantive-reference testing remain distinct, with an actual-context ethics/IRB/
    legal determination required before reader recruitment; and
11. no Phase A/B execution, recruitment, engineering, or product-state change is authorized.

## Discipline

- Review the frozen v2.1 artifacts, v2-to-v2.1 changelog, adjudication, and neutral context.
- Do not reopen findings already resolved in v2 without new evidence or an internal contradiction.
- Distinguish a true closure defect from a preference or future Phase A execution detail.
- Source every external factual assertion; place unsourced concerns in “Suspicions I could not
  source.”
- Do not require humans merely because they are conventional, and do not accept a nonhuman anchor
  merely because it is cheaper.
- Do not contact the project or request internal hostile reviews/conversation history.

## Output

Lead with exactly one:

- `CLOSURE_CONFIRMED_READY_FOR_OWNER_COSTING_DECISION`
- `CLOSURE_REQUIRES_NARROW_FIXES`
- `HUMAN_REFERENCE_ALREADY_SHOWN_INDISPENSABLE`
- `VALIDATION_PLAN_STILL_NOT_CREDIBLE`

Then provide:

1. N-1–N-6 closure table with exact citations.
2. Human-reference decision-point audit.
3. Costing/envelope/reserve audit.
4. Any new blocking or material finding, with the smallest adequate fix.
5. “Suspicions I could not source,” if any.

If N-1–N-6 are closed and no new blocking/material contradiction exists, say so and stop. Do not
manufacture another design cycle.
