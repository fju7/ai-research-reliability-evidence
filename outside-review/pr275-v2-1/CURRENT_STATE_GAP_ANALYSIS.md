## 1. Executive finding

WHU has a substantial mechanical substrate and no demonstrated product claim. The independent v1
review identified a more basic defect than missing controls: the planned measurement instrument
could not support its central verdict. The reader was absent, the reliability calculation assumed
unsupported independence, the comparator was unspecified, and the terms governing pass/fail were
undefined and authored at the gate they controlled.

The sequencing therefore changes. The next potential program work is not the v1 operational G1.
It is a measurement-instrument freeze followed, only with separate authorization, by a cheap
Epistemic Feasibility Probe over the retained aspirin material. Major engineering remains paused.

The same reviewer's v2 disposition was `READY_WITH_MATERIAL_FIXES`. It found the v1 blockers closed
and identified six local defects: cost the validation design before envelope selection; reserve
Phase B funds; separate validation affordability from product economics; narrow the favorable probe
label; close the independence-equivalence escape hatch; make Phase F fail; and independently
challenge shared instrument/system lineage. V2.1 addresses these without redesigning the gates.

Current supported state:

> unmeasured; qualified-reference feasibility unknown; evidence adequacy `NOT_ASSESSED`; no

## 3. Evidence worth preserving

### 3.1 Mechanical substrate

The base includes immutable identities/receipts, encrypted custody, provenance, native-source and
representation objects, dependency invalidation, bounded review/authorization objects,
release/public-state reconciliation, surveillance contracts, deterministic controls, typed failure
states, and tests. These are valuable controls. They are not product-validation evidence.

### 3.2 Aspirin evidence already in hand

The repository reports 11 SHA/custody-verified retained PDFs, 11 exact publication identities, and
124 structured SHADOW evidence records. At the reported checkpoint 115 records had native page
locators and ten remained unresolved. Independent extraction validation over nine documents found
broad classification stability but zero exact reported-estimate overlap. That adverse result is the
most decision-relevant existing product evidence and must remain visible.

### 3.3 Strong v1 gate properties

V1 G3 had concrete extraction failure conditions and retained hard failures in denominators. V1 G7
could falsify genericity through a materially different domain and rejected pooled averages that
hide regression. V1 G8 contained conditional and negative economic outcomes. V2 preserves those
properties in Phases D, G, and H.

## 4. Product-validation gaps

### 4.1 Intended-reader effect

No evidence shows what sophisticated non-experts understand or believe after reading a WHU
assessment. Artifact qualifications and expert approval cannot substitute for measured reader
calibration. The v1 not-ship condition for confusing process evidence with truth had no trigger.

### 4.2 Qualified independent reference

No protected, qualified, independent end-to-end aspirin synthesis reference or adjudication result
exists. Prior model-family comparisons demonstrated instability, not correctness. With only one
internal human, independence is a staffing/economic feasibility question, not a role label.

### 4.3 Statistical identification

The v1 fixed-cluster criterion did not define the unit of analysis or represent common-mode
dependencies from shared corpus, adapter, protocol, model, rubric, reference, evaluator, and
implementer. There is no justified replacement sample size today. A prospective statistical design
and achievable-information analysis are missing.

### 4.4 Comparator

No frozen commercially meaningful simpler workflow exists. Operator qualifications, tools, access,
resource matching, output/audit requirements, fairness approval, and decision margins are missing.
Without them, relative benefit and cost cannot be interpreted.

### 4.5 Load-bearing definitions

`Severe`, `consequential`, `catastrophic subgroup`, `useful coverage`, `material change`, `false
assurance`, and `load-bearing` lack a frozen independently approved operational rubric with positive
and negative examples.

### 4.6 Coverage and defect realism

Aggregate coverage can hide abstention on difficult or consequential cases. Planted defects can be
made easier than naturalistic failures. No frozen stratification or calibration protocol currently
prevents either failure.

### 4.7 Full economics and termination

V1 excluded or separated likely dominant costs: reference construction/refresh, expert
adjudication, human labor, document acquisition, subscriptions, and maintenance. No total program
budget, calendar, or retry ceiling existed. Therefore the program could remain compliant while
building indefinitely.

### 4.8 Validation anchors and human necessity

V2 assumed multiple externally approved human roles before it established which target properties
could instead be validated by formal, executable, authoritative-structured, protected-known-answer,
outcome-based, or professional-method anchors. It also asked Fred to choose an envelope before the
roles were costed. The missing evidence is now a Validation Anchor / Role Necessity Map and
low/base/high costing worksheet. Neither a large human panel nor a nonhuman substitute is presumed.
When a named property genuinely requires qualified human judgment, the program stops at an explicit
human-reference decision rather than weakening the reference or remaining indefinitely unassessed.

Validation-study affordability and repeatable product economics are separate unknowns. Failure to
fund a credible study at this project's scale cannot establish that the product itself is
uneconomic.

## 6. Early-probe feasibility unknowns

Before the probe can run, the project must establish:

- which bounded question the retained corpus can legitimately support;
- whether qualified reference constructors and adjudicators can be recruited independently;
- their qualifications, conflicts, price, availability, and time;
- whether reference creation can be maintained after material evidence or scope changes;
- whether a reader sample and valid calibration instrument fit the program envelope;
- whether the comparator represents the actual commercial alternative;
- whether the common dependencies permit a defensible inference at project scale; and
- whether full cost already makes the product economically implausible.

Failure to resolve any of these is itself an early disposition. V2 does not disguise it as future
implementation work.
