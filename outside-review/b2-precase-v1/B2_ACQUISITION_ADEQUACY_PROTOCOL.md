# B2 acquisition-adequacy protocol

**State:** `FROZEN_PRE_CASE`  
**Frozen:** 2026-09-21  
**Authority:** SHADOW research only; no case, search, provider call, acquisition, synthesis, publication,
production, or subscriber action is authorized by this protocol.

## Target property and claim ceiling

B2 asks whether WHU, given only a bounded research question and its declared scope, can independently
discover, identify, acquire, bind, and appropriately disposition the evidence that matters well enough
to support a defensible bounded synthesis, without access to the protected professional reference
backbone.

Acquisition adequacy is case-, scope-, cutoff-, route-, and intended-use-specific. It is not universal
completeness, provider count, route count, document volume, low marginal yield, convergence, or agreement
with one review treated as omniscient truth. `PROTOCOL_COMPLETE` never implies `EVIDENCE_COMPLETE` or
`ACQUISITION_ADEQUATE`.

The evaluation reports separately:

- document/report recall;
- study, dataset, authority, or source-family recall;
- required evidence-class coverage;
- pivotal/load-bearing protected-source recall;
- candidate-blind counterevidence recall;
- correction, update, replacement, and retraction recall;
- registry, ongoing, and unpublished-evidence discovery where applicable;
- inaccessible-but-identified evidence;
- exact source-family and version identity; and
- consequential-evidence recall.

Raw document recall and consequential recall are never substituted for one another.

## Prospective sequence

1. Freeze and hash this package and obtain the narrow independent pre-case review described below.
2. After that review closes and any accepted changes are re-frozen, apply the frozen case criteria
   without revealing protected material to the acquisition side.
3. Custody creates the protected reference package and a public identity-free commitment receipt.
4. Acquisition receives only the bounded question, scope, population, jurisdiction, timeframe, use,
   routes, stopping rule, and budgets.
5. B2A executes acquisition and seals its output before evaluation unblinds.
6. Evaluation resolves equivalence, scores B2A, and records reference limitations and WHU-only finds.
7. B2B may run only under the prospectively permitted trigger in the consequential-evidence method.

No post-freeze route, query family, budget, equivalence rule, label, or threshold may be changed after
case identity or protected outcomes are visible. A necessary material change ends the run as
`B2_NOT_INTERPRETABLE`; it may inform a separately authorized successor protocol.

## Frozen acquisition protocol

Evidence classes are declared before providers. For the selected case, the case-selection record must
map each applicable class to a materially different discovery structure and mark it `REQUIRED`,
`OPTIONAL`, or `NOT_APPLICABLE` with a reason. The minimum classes to assess are primary studies or
authorities; systematic/professional synthesis; first-party guideline, regulator, HTA, or issuer
material; harms/contrary evidence; applicability/qualification; corrections/updates/retractions; and,
where applicable, registries, ongoing/unpublished work and grey evidence.

Required route families are: topical scholarly/domain-index search; first-party authoritative search;
systematic-synthesis discovery; candidate-blind counterevidence search; backward and forward citation
traversal; correction/retraction/update traversal; and registry/ongoing search where applicable.
General-web discovery is required only when the selected domain has relevant evidence not adequately
represented by the other structures. Provider substitutions must preserve the route's evidence
structure and be recorded; a failed route remains failed.

Every candidate receives a durable route/query/date receipt and a disposition. Exact publication,
version, correction status, native-object binding, access state, and source-family relationship are
resolved where possible. Inaccessible candidates enter the manual-retrieval queue; the experiment does
not purchase sources or contact authors without later owner authority.

## Stopping rule

Acquisition stops only after all of the following are terminally recorded:

1. every required route is `EXECUTED`, `ERROR`, or `UNAVAILABLE`;
2. every frozen query family has completed its initial pass and at most two refinement iterations;
3. backward and forward traversal reaches depth two from each included pivotal seed, or a recorded
   route/access/budget boundary;
4. candidate-blind counterevidence and correction/update/retraction passes are complete;
5. every known gap and inaccessible candidate is dispositioned;
6. the earlier of the frozen case-specific wall-clock, human-time, provider-cost, and candidate-screening
   ceilings is reached only after required safety/custody finalization; and
7. the declared evidence cutoff is applied.

Marginal unique yield is reported by route and iteration but cannot stop the run by itself. The stopping
record establishes only `PROTOCOL_COMPLETE` or `PROTOCOL_INCOMPLETE`.

## B2A and B2B

**B2A** is the mandatory acquisition-only comparison against the protected reference. It preserves all
misses regardless of later synthesis impact.

**B2B** is optional downstream sensitivity testing and may begin only after B2A is sealed and only when
the protected reference does not independently resolve whether a miss or WHU-only discovery could
materially change or qualify the bounded synthesis. B2B compares deterministic or protected
professional synthesis conclusions where a valid common construct exists. It cannot erase, rescore, or
hide B2A recall failures. No B2A or B2B execution is authorized now.

## Governance and independent review gate

A narrow outside review is required before case selection because route design, equivalence, scoring,
and consequentiality are vulnerable to benchmark-design bias. The reviewer receives this entire
pre-case package, no case identity, no protected corpus, and no internal adjudication or gate report.
The question is limited to whether the frozen design can validly test bounded acquisition adequacy
without leakage, circular scoring, or case-selection gaming. Review closure and adjudication must be
committed before case selection. No reviewer contact is authorized by this freeze.

## Boundaries

All outputs remain append-only SHADOW records. Publication/release consumers must reject them as
authority. Copyright, provider terms, access control, encrypted native custody, raw-response retention,
and deletion policies remain applicable. There is no open-world completeness claim, autonomous
adequacy label, human recruitment, source purchase, production path, or subscriber-facing result.
