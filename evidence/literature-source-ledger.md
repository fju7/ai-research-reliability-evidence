## Ledger conclusions

- **68 unique records** were retained: 44 primary empirical/theoretical papers, 11 standards/guidelines, 9 official technical reports, and 4 scholarly reviews/positioning sources (classification overlaps resolved by primary role).
- **49 were inspected in full or through official full text; 19 were metadata/abstract level only.** No proposition that depends solely on an inaccessible source is used as a dispositive finding.
- Recent 2025–2026 work is identified as preprint/technical report where applicable. URLs point to DOI, official publication, or official project pages; no search-result snippets are cited as evidence.
- The decisive negative finding is not that prior fields “solve” open-world research. It is that most proposed engineering controls and several conceptual labels are mature, so novelty cannot rest on rediscovering lineage, state, assurance, common-mode failure, automation bias, search flow accounting, or test-oracle fallibility.
