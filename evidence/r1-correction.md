# R1 public evidence presentation

R1 is a correction and research-process record, not evidence for the thesis. It was previously considered one of our strongest semantic-transformation examples. Final primary-source reconstruction found that characterization incorrect, and the example was removed before publication.

The source reports KEYNOTE-942 as intismeran plus pembrolizumab versus pembrolizumab alone. Its five-year release first uses the phrase “attributed to intismeran autogene in combination with KEYTRUDA,” then states that most events “attributed to intismeran autogene” were Grade 1 or 2. WHU revised a sentence about mild-to-moderate side effects to say they were attributed to the vaccine.

Because the source itself supplies component-specific attribution language, the public record does not support our earlier claim that WHU processing invented that attribution from arm-level observations. The trial lacks an intismeran-only arm, so the source also should not be read as randomized causal isolation of the component. Both points matter.

Result: the example was removed from the essay. See [the full reconstruction](r1-independent-reconstruction.md) and [second-route verification](r1-second-route-verification.md).
