# Source-native reference-construct validation — synthesis and closeout

## Final disposition

`SOURCE_NATIVE_REFERENCE_ROUTE_NOT_JUSTIFIED`

This disposition applies to the recruited corpus and current execution route. It does not prove that no future source-native route can work. It means this run cannot justify consumer-experiment design, and its defects cannot be repaired by reclassifying, resealing, or extending the existing corpus.

## Recruitment result

The study screened exactly 24 consecutive source-native candidates and included all 24: eight IETF errata, eight CISA KEV entries, and eight FDA drug-recall enforcement records. Exclusions were zero. The preregistered stopping rule was reached normally with no outcome-directed search extension. Allocation was 9 development and 15 confirmation, with three and five respectively from each population.

A prospective amendment was necessary when the first CISA snapshot showed that BOD 26-04 had superseded BOD 22-01. The amendment was committed before extraction or classification and did not change recruitment, allocation, gates, or desired composition.

## Reference findings

Independent rule provenance sources were captured for 24/24 candidates. This was a provenance success but not a determination success. The nine development-only source-grounded extractions produced:

- D1: 6 `INDETERMINATE`, 3 `WITHHOLD`, 0 `AUTHORIZE`;
- D2: 3 `UNDERDETERMINED`, 6 `CONTRADICTED`;
- D3: 6 `OPEN_SPECIFIED_DISCRIMINATOR`, 3 `SETTLED_FOR_BOUNDED_QUESTION`;
- D4-RV: 9 `NOT_ESTABLISHED`;
- D4-RS: 9 `NOT_ESTABLISHED`.

The three IETF development records were newly reported technical errata whose verification remained open. The three CISA development records had official required-action text but lacked the asset-specific BOD 26-04 predicates needed for a determinate authorization warrant. The three FDA development records were ongoing recalls, yielding determinate withholding for continued ordinary distribution.

LIO authentication succeeded for 0/24 and failed to establish for 24/24. The sources preserve current record content, but the study could not authenticate the constructed object's version, parentage, and evidence membership under the frozen two-observation/cryptographic standard. D4 therefore remained operationally unestablished rather than being inferred from plausible metadata.

## Confirmation failure

During apparatus diagnostics, confirmation evidence fields were displayed with allocation labels. Under the frozen leakage rule, all 15 confirmation items were invalidated. They were not replaced, reallocated, resealed, or classified. The usable confirmation denominator is 0, so agreement, grounding, common-mode sensitivity, and external-validity estimates are not available.

The pre-execution audit was consequently `NOT_READY_NO_CALLS_AUTHORIZED`. Reference-adjudication calls were 0, provider preflight calls were 0, consumer/intervention calls were 0, errors were 0, and retries were 0. No response freeze was fabricated.

## Controls, challenges, and readiness

Naturally certified Class S specificity controls: 0. Naturally certified Class F false-reassurance challenges: 0. No consumer behavior was observed or used. Development contained no `AUTHORIZE` result, every D4 result was unestablished, and confirmation was unavailable. The corpus was not extended or rewritten to fix this composition.

The validation gates fail because there is no meaningful sealed confirmation subset, no authenticated LIO record, no demonstrated D4 revelation/resolution distinction, no independent adjudicator path, and neither required treatment-blind effect class. Proceeding to consumer design would turn these missing reference conditions into assumptions.

## Evidence for and against external validity

The strongest evidence for the construct is procedural: heterogeneous official source populations were recruited consecutively; all screened records were retained; snapshots, access times, identifiers, and hashes permit reconstruction; and rule provenance was captured independently of desired classifications.

The strongest evidence against external validity is substantive: source-native origin did not make the LIO facts authenticatable, did not yield a natural authorization-appropriate control in development, and did not survive sealed confirmation. The available machine-only path would also lack the human-led or ancestry-diverse external reference required by the gate.

## Claim ceiling and next implication

This run supports only a bounded feasibility conclusion: independent public records and domain rules can populate parts of D1–D3, while D4 and confirmation integrity remain decisive bottlenecks. It does not validate D1–D4, measure consumer effects, establish general reliability, prove the authorities correct, or support cross-domain causal claims.

A future attempt would require a new preregistration; genuinely new consecutive records; role-separated sealing that prevents the apparatus developer from seeing confirmation evidence; sources with cryptographically verifiable or independently replicated version/parent history; and a human-led or demonstrably ancestry-diverse reference path. The 15 contaminated confirmation cases may be used only as development material in such a future study.

No consumer experiment was designed or executed. No paper chapter, evidence map, findings file, or outline was changed because the phase did not produce validated evidence. The old 30 worlds were not reused confirmatorily. Protected historical artifacts and `.DS_Store` were untouched.
